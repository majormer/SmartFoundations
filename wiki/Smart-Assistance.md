# Smart Assistance

Use **Toggle Smart (Session)** when you want to place normally for a while without editing saved mod settings.

## Turn Assistance Off or On

1. Press **Num Decimal**, or your rebound Toggle Smart (Session) key.
2. Disabling cancels the current Smart preview. Select a building or blueprint recipe again.
3. Build using normal Satisfactory placement.
4. Press the toggle again to re-enable Smart assistance.

The choice survives recipe changes and holstering for the current session. It does not overwrite your saved grid, Auto-Connect, or Extend configuration. Each multiplayer player controls their own assistance.

## Large Vanilla Blueprints

Smart construction has a safe payload limit. Disabling assistance lets you select a large vanilla blueprint again and place it normally, without Smart's extra grid or seam connections. It does not increase the limit for Smart placements or provide free materials.

## Rebind the Key

Find **Toggle Smart (Session)** in **Options > Keybindings > Smart! Mod Controls**. Choose any convenient key if your keyboard has no numpad. The action remains available while assistance is off.

Double-tapping Num 0 is a separate Auto-Connect/Extend shortcut; it does not replace this toggle.
