# Quick Start

This is the fastest way to make sure Smart! is working.

## Place Your First Smart Grid

1. Equip a 4m foundation in the build gun.
2. Press `K` to open the Smart Panel.
3. Set the grid to `3 x 3 x 1`.
4. Press **Apply** if placement edits are staged, then close the Panel.
5. Check the preview in the world.
6. Click once to build the full grid.

Smart! will charge the normal material cost for all 9 foundations.

![Apply the nine-foundation grid before closing the Panel](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/wiki/majormer/SmartFoundations/images/v35/scaling.png)

## Try The Keyboard Flow

With a buildable hologram active:

- Press `Num 8` or `Num 5` to increase or decrease the active axis (X by default).
- Press `Num 6` or `Num 4` to adjust Y.
- Press `Num 9` or `Num 3` to adjust Z.
- Press `Num 0` to change which axis `Num 8` / `Num 5` adjust.

If you prefer mouse wheel control, hold a Smart modifier:

- Hold `X` and scroll to adjust X.
- Hold `Z` and scroll to adjust Y.
- Hold `X + Z` and scroll to adjust Z.

## Find the other tools

The **Scaling / Walking / Upgrade / Restore** tabs share one window. Tasks depend on the held buildable. Hover a **?** beside the building or section for help, or click it to expand the instructions. A disabled task explains what to select first.

For an existing pipe bus, hold **Stackable Pipeline Support**, aim at an open endpoint and choose Walking. Holding the pipe itself opens different tools. See [resume steps](Smart-Walking).

## Good First Experiments

- Make a `10 x 10` foundation pad.
- Build a row of constructors with extra spacing.
- Use Steps to make a ramp-like foundation pattern.
- Use Auto-Connect with a small row of splitters and machines.
- Scale one of your own blueprints into a small grid and watch the belts/pipes wire between the copies ([Smart Blueprints](Smart-Blueprints)).
- Use Extend after you have one working production module.
- Try [Smart Walking](Smart-Walking) for a connected belt, pipe, or hypertube run that turns and climbs.

## Build Normally For a While

Press **Num Decimal** to disable Smart building assistance for this session, then select a recipe again. You can place normally without changing saved settings. Press the key again to re-enable assistance. See [Smart Assistance](Smart-Assistance), including how to rebind the toggle.

## What Smart! Does Not Do

Smart! does not create free buildings or resources. If you do not have enough materials, the build will fail just like a normal Satisfactory build.
