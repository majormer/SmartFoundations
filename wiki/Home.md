# Smart! Wiki

Smart! helps you build bigger, cleaner factories in Satisfactory with fewer repeated clicks.

Use it when you want to place a grid of foundations, line up machines, copy a working production block, auto-connect logistics, or upgrade a lot of belts and pipes after unlocking a better tier.

Smart! still uses normal Satisfactory buildings and normal material costs. It is a building helper, not a creative-mode or free-build mod.

## Main Features

- [Smart Assistance](Smart-Assistance): temporarily turn Smart building tools off for your session.
- [Grid Scaling](Grid-Scaling): place many copies of the same buildable at once.
- [Transforms](Transforms): add spacing, steps, stagger, or curved layouts.
- [Auto-Connect](Auto-Connect): preview belts, pipes, Hypertubes, and power lines automatically.
- [Smart Blueprints](Smart-Blueprints): scale your own blueprints into a grid, wired together.
- [Extend](Extend): copy an existing working factory module.
- [Smart Walking](Smart-Walking): lay or resume a connected belt, pipe, Hypertube, railway or Vehicle Path route.
- [Smart Restore](Smart-Restore): save, share, and replay Smart! setups and whole module layouts.
- [Smart Upgrade](Smart-Upgrade): upgrade or downgrade belts, lifts, and pipes, or upgrade power poles, in batches.
- [Smart Dismantle](Smart-Dismantle): dismantle Smart-built groups through vanilla Blueprint Dismantle.
- [Smart Camera](Smart-Camera): companion picture-in-picture views with live layout editing and save scopes.
