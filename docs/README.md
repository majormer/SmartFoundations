## Native conveyor research

The canonical paper lives in **Reference**, alongside the contracts developers
must preserve. Keeping its existing path preserves source and documentation links.

- [Native conveyor, chain-actor, construction and replication research](Reference/THESIS_Belts_ChainActors_Placement.md)
- [Historical conveyor evidence and superseded interpretations](Reference/THESIS_Belts_ChainActors_Placement_Historical.md)
- [Extend, Restore and Upgrade compliance plan](Development/PLAN_ConveyorContractCompliance.md)

The paper distinguishes recovered native mechanisms, observed runtime behavior,
Smart source findings, and unresolved limits. Its Linux observations include
transport, replacement, persistence, client reconstruction and controlled segment
multipart completion. They do not certify Smart's implementation. The historical
record preserves crash signatures and do-not-retest boundaries; use the current
paper for conclusions.
